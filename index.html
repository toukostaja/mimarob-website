carries the context.

Palomar shows players what needs to happen next and gives operators the facts behind it. When a player contacts support, both sides start with the same context.

  1. 01

    Player requests a withdrawal

    When the missing KYC documents prevent the approval, the player is immediately asked to upload exactly what is required.

  2. 02

HOW PALOMAR DECIDES

Rules where it must be exact.
AI where it helps.
People where it matters.

Not every task in iGaming operations should be carried out in the same way. Palomar delegates each kind of task to the layer that does it best, and keeps it there.

Rules decide what is allowed

Payments, limits, verification and compliance follow explicit rules. The same situation gets the same answer, with a reason an operator can read and an auditor can trace. When rules conflict, the safer outcome wins.

AI reads and points the way

AI handles what rules can't: reading long conversations in several languages, picking out what the next person needs to know and flagging where a human should look. Every point links back to its source. It never moves money, changes an account or messages a player.

People make the judgement calls

Safer-play interventions, fraud and compliance decisions, and exceptions to a rule stay with the responsible operator, with the context in front of them and a second approval where policy requires one.

IN PRACTICE

Four new accounts. One device.

  1. Rules catch the known pattern

    When an account registers, Palomar compares it with existing ones. A shared device and a shared recent IP address match the screening rules, so the four accounts are grouped into one case for review, with each rule's evidence attached.

  2. AI looks wider

    Rules only catch patterns someone has written down. AI reviews the accounts' wider activity and summarises what stands out: all four claimed the same welcome offer within an hour and requested withdrawals as soon as wagering was met. It points to the evidence; it doesn't label anyone.

  3. The fraud analyst decides

    The analyst can confirm the link, dismiss it as a false positive, for example a shared household, or escalate it to compliance. Their decision, not the AI summary, is what restricts or clears the accounts, and it's recorded with their reason.
