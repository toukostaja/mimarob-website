PALOMAR

More of the operation.
One connected product.

Palomar is being built to bring player journeys, support, CRM, affiliate operations and the work around payments and games into one system. More of the essentials in one product means fewer separate tools to buy, connect and manage. Specialist services can connect where they add value.

THE PRODUCT IDEA

The platform
carries the context.

Palomar shows players what needs to happen next and gives operators the facts behind it. When a player contacts support, both sides start with the same context.

  1. 01

    Player requests a withdrawal

    When the missing KYC documents prevent the approval, the player is immediately asked to upload exactly what is required.

  2. 02

    Player uploads the documents

    The player takes a picture of the documents, submits them, and decides to contact the support for an update and a quicker review.

  3. 03

    Operator receives the support case

    The support tool shows the blocked withdrawal and the document review cases. The agent has all context and features to make the decisions and unblock the player without asking for additional questions.
